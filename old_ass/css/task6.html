Rivers of India

GANGA

The Ganga is the longest and most holy river in India. It starts from the Gangotri Glacier in the state of Uttarakhand. It flows across northern India and empties into the Bay of Bengal. Millions of people depend on it for water and daily life.

GODOVARI

The Godavari is the second-longest river in India and the longest in South India. People often call it the "Dakshin Ganga" or the Southern Ganga. It starts near Trimbak in the state of Maharashtra and flows east into the Bay of Bengal.

BRAHMAPUTRA

The Brahmaputra is a very wide and deep river in the northeast of India. It starts in Tibet as the Yarlung Tsangpo before entering India through Arunachal Pradesh. It brings rich soil to the Assam valley and joins the sea in Bangladesh

YAMUNA

The Yamuna is the biggest helper stream (tributary) of the Ganga. It begins at the Yamunotri Glacier in Uttarakhand. It flows past big cities like Delhi and Agra before it meets the Ganga river at a holy place called Prayagraj.

NARMADA

The Narmada is a special river because it flows to the west instead of the east. It starts at the Amarkantak Plateau in Madhya Pradesh. It runs through rocky valleys and empties into the Arabian Sea.
