Seethalakshmi Ramaswami College

Seethalakshmi Ramaswami College, popularly known as SRC, is a women's college located in Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu.

The college offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes in Arts, Science and Commerce. It is affiliated with Bharathidasan University and has been accredited with an A+ grade by NAAC.

SRC provides students with facilities such as a library, laboratories, IT infrastructure, sports facilities, auditorium and hostel facilities. The college also provides opportunities for students to develop their academic, technical and professional skills.

The college is located at Sankaran Pillai Road, Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu - 620002.
